Pillars

Protecting Public Education

Our children deserve more than just classrooms—they deserve safe, well-funded learning environments that empower them. I will advocate for legislation that increases public school funding, expands educational resources, and prioritizes student safety. Children are not just our future, but also my constituents and they deserve to be considered.

Learn more

Strengthening Rural Communities

Many of our cites are facing economic challenges and are in dire need of support. Businesses are closing and new opportunities are scarce. I will work hand-in-hand with local leadership to revitalize our economy, attract investment, and support sustainable growth across the district.

Learn more

Championing Farmers and Small Businesses

District 38 is home to hardworking farmers and many entrepreneurs who are the backbone of our rural economies. Finding ways to bring in larger business can help support our more local atmosphere and bring more people into our community. I understand the challenges - from labor shortages to unfair compensation- and I will fight to ensure farmers receive the support and recognition they deserve.
